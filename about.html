About Time Zone Meeting Planner

Time Zone Meeting Planner is a lightweight browser-based utility designed to help people in different cities find practical meeting times. It compares local working hours for up to three cities on a shared timeline.

How it works

The tool uses your browser’s built-in time-zone support and IANA time-zone identifiers to calculate local times. It does not require an account or a calculation backend. Future results depend on the time-zone data available in your browser and may be affected by government rule changes.

Our approach

We aim to keep the tool simple, accessible on desktop and mobile, and clear about its limitations. The tool is provided for planning assistance; please verify important appointments with your calendar.
