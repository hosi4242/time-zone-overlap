Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Information processed by the tool

Time Zone Meeting Planner performs time-zone calculations in your browser. The site does not require registration and does not send your selected cities or meeting settings to a calculation server. If you choose to copy or share a link, the settings encoded in that link may be visible to anyone who receives it.

Local browser storage

The site may use browser local storage to remember your language preference. This information remains in your browser and can be removed by clearing the site’s browser data.

Hosting and technical logs

The hosting provider may process standard technical information needed to deliver and protect the website, such as request details, IP address, browser information, and timestamps, according to the provider’s own policies and retention practices.

Cookies, analytics, and advertising

This site uses Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use the website and to help improve the tool. Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies and process usage information such as page views, interactions, browser and device details, referral information, and approximate geographic information. Google processes analytics data under its own terms and privacy policies. You can limit or clear cookies through your browser settings. For more information, please review Google’s Privacy Policy and how Google Analytics safeguards data. This site does not currently intentionally embed Google AdSense or other advertising scripts; this policy will be updated if advertising services are added.

External websites

Links to external websites are governed by the privacy policies of those websites. We do not control their practices.

Policy updates

This policy may be updated when the site’s functionality or service providers change. The revision date above will be updated when material changes are made.
